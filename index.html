A lost animal is a lost livelihood

Strays happen

Cattle and buffalo wander off grazing land, into fields, roads or forest edges — often noticed only hours later.

Flocks scatter

Sheep and goats graze over wide, open areas, making it hard to keep count and spot one that has drifted away.

Searching costs time

Hours spent searching on foot is time away from work. Knowing the last known location changes everything.

Built for the field

What the Gocharan app does today with simulated collars — ready for real hardware.

🗺️ Live map with freshness

Every animal on one map, labelled LIVE, RECENT or STALE so you know how fresh each location is.

📐 Grazing-area geofences

Draw circle or polygon grazing areas around your land and assign them to your herd.

🔔 Exit & return alerts

Get notified when an animal leaves a grazing area — and again when it comes back.

🔋 Battery & offline alerts

Low collar battery, a collar that stopped reporting, back online, or poor GPS signal.

🧭 History trails

Replay where an animal has walked, and export location history as CSV.

How it works

  1. 1

    Collar

    A GPS collar on each animal records its position and battery level.

  2. 2

    Cellular

    The collar sends readings securely over the mobile network — no Wi‑Fi needed in the field.

  3. 3

    App

    Gocharan checks geofences, updates the live map and alerts you on your phone.

FAQ

When will Gocharan be available?

It's coming soon. The app is in development and the collar hardware is still being built and tested. No launch date is fixed yet.

How much will the collar cost?

Pricing is not final. We are aiming to make the collar affordable for small and marginal farmers.

Which animals does it support?

Cattle, buffalo, sheep and goats.

Does it need internet in the field?

The collar uses the cellular (mobile) network to send locations. Your phone needs data to receive updates.

Which languages are supported?

English and Telugu, with more Indian languages planned.
